# Sandwich Construction

## Assumptions
- We have an unobstructued countertop
- On the countertop we have:
    - A plate with two slices of bread side-by-side
    - Jar of Peanut Butter
    - Jar of jelly
    - A Butterknife
    - Paper towel
    - The robot is charged and ready to go
    <br><br><br>

    ## Steps to build the sandwich
    1. Open the Peanut Butter
    2. Open the Jelly
    3. Pick up the butterknife
    4. Use the butterknife to scoop peanut butter out of the jar
    5. Spread Peanut butter on the slice of bread on the left
    6. Use the paper towel to clean the butterknife
    7. Use the butterknife to scoop jelly out of the jar
    8. Spread Jelly on the slice of bread on the right
    9. Stack the bread with Peanut Butter and Jelly sides touching
    10. Use the paper towel to clean the butterknife
    11. Discard paper towel
    12. Put the butterknife down on the countertop
    13. Replace the lids on the Peanut Butter & Jelly
    14. Put Peanut Butter, Jelly, and knife away

     <br><br><br>

     ## Exit Conditions
     - The plate and the constructed sandiwch are the only items on the countertop
